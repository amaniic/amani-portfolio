Help

Build your collection

Choose Add, fill out the required fields, and select Save. Enter the budget as a number in US dollars, without commas or an M suffix.

Find and modify movies

Show all lists your movies and their IDs. Open a card for details. Modify and Display info also let you look up a movie by ID.

Prevent duplicates

The combination of title, director, and release year must be unique. Capitalization and repeated whitespace do not create a different movie.

Posters and errors

Posters are optional local images. If a field is invalid, correct the form and save again. An unknown ID shows a not-found page.

Delete

Open a movie and select Delete. Confirm to remove it permanently.
